This animation shows the Pacific Ocean daily surface temperature anomaly during May to October 2026, compared to a reference period from 1991 to 2020.

El Niño is caused by the weakening of trade winds across the Pacific Ocean. The winds usually blow from east to west, pushing warm surface water westwards. The weakened winds mean that warm surface water accumulates in the eastern Pacific Ocean.
