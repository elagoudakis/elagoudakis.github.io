## Who

My name is Manos, and I am a remote sensing specialist with over 15 years of professional experience in the geospatial domain.

For the majority of my career, I have worked as a contractor at the European Space Agency (ESA), delivering high-quality data visualizations to a wide audience through the Agency's outreach channels.

## What

**DScape** stands for **Digital Scape**: a digital representation of what's happening around us.

DScape is the realization of a personal project. In this digital repo, expect to find a variety of content, including visual samples of past work split into snapshots and longer reads, quick tutorials, and my takes relevant to the geospatial ecosystem.

You might find some of the topics interesting, or maybe not. Either way, feel free to share your thoughts. I'll also try to answer any technical questions you might have regarding processing and methodology.
